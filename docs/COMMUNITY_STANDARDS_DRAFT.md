# Safari Circle community standards — review draft

Safari Circle exists for respectful safari travellers, guides, photographers, filmmakers, researchers and conservation practitioners. Members must credit creative work, respect people and communities, and protect wildlife.

Prohibited conduct includes harassment, threats, hate, impersonation, fraud, spam, trafficking, poaching assistance, deliberate wildlife disturbance, doxxing, sexual exploitation, illegal sales and publication of sensitive wildlife locations that creates foreseeable harm. Dangerous field or travel claims may be labelled, reduced in distribution or removed.

Members can report accounts, posts and messages, block other members and appeal enforcement decisions. Moderators record a reason and decision trail. Urgent credible threats to people or wildlife follow the operator's escalation procedure.

This policy requires final legal review, moderator training, appeal service levels and named escalation contacts before public launch.
