# African Safari Pocketbook privacy policy — review draft

Last revised: 15 September 2026

African Safari Pocketbook provides offline safari references, trip planning, a private field journal and the Safari Circle community. Journal entries and deliberately saved offline media remain on the device unless the user exports or shares them. Circle account details, posts, messages, follows, blocks, reports and preferences are processed by the Circle service to provide the community.

The Circle service is designed to collect the minimum account information needed for identity, age eligibility, contact verification, security and community safety. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Session cookies are HTTP-only, secure and same-site. Precise wildlife locations are protected by default. Reports are available only to authorised moderators and are retained for safety, appeals and legal obligations.

Users may request access, correction, export or deletion of account information. A production publication must replace this paragraph with the operator's verified legal name, physical/contact address, privacy email, applicable retention periods, lawful bases, international-transfer safeguards, child-safety terms and jurisdiction-specific rights.

The app opens authoritative external services for live travel, health, map, biodiversity and media information. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Live advisory and health responses are never stored in the Pocketbook application cache.

This document is a product-complete draft requiring review and approval by qualified counsel before public release.
